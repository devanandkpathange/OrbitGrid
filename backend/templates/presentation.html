Mathematical Core

Dual-Engine Siting: Fermat-Weber & CFLP

Continuous geometric center-of-gravity calculation coupled with discrete integer programming constraints, executing in sub-3 milliseconds.

Continuous Fermat-Weber Solver (Weiszfeld)
Minimizes weighted ton-kilometer Euclidean distance across all demand clusters:
min ∑ (w_i · dist(X, P_i) · c_km)
Capacitated Facility Location (CFLP)
Enforces operational boundaries, maximum warehouse capacity ceilings (e.g. 35,000 units), and strict SLA delivery radius limits.
∑ d_i ≤ Capacity_j | dist_ij ≤ Max_SLA

CFO Intelligence

Pareto Frontier: The Warehouse Sweet Spot

How many warehouses should a business open? Every new facility reduces transit distance but increases fixed rent. Orbit Grid solves the exact commercial inflection point ($k=1 \dots 5$).

The Diminishing Return Inflection Curve
• k=1 to k=2: Slashes freight by 32% with minimal rent addition.
• k=2 to k=3: Reaches the Golden Pareto Sweet Spot (lowest total landed cost).
• k > 4: Diminishing returns set in (fixed lease costs outstrip transport savings).
